Renewals desk

Built-in
Relationship OS · version 1.4.0 · updated 28 Oct · 1,240 installs

For anyone who looks after existing customers. Every Monday you get the renewals due in the next 90 days. Ask for a renewal brief on any account and get the deal history, open issues and contacts on one page. Includes a renewals dashboard and a saved view.

What it adds

Skills · 2

  • Prepare a renewal briefDeal history, open issues and who signs
  • Draft a renewal emailUses your writing style; you send it

Routines · 2

  • Monday renewals round-upMondays 8:00 am. Tried on 30 days: 4 messages
  • Renewal reply watchWhen a customer renewing within 60 days emails you
Added to My routines, where you can pause them.

Job profiles · 1

  • Renewal researcherReads organisations, deals and your emails with them. Up to $1.00 a run.

Saved code and chat cards · 2

  • Upcoming renewalsBuilds the round-up card straight from your data
  • Renewal brief card

Dashboard and view · 2

  • Renewals dashboardYour own copy. Share it if you like.
  • Deals: renewals in the next 90 days

Fields it uses

  • Renewal date on DealsAlready in your workspace

What's new in 1.4.0

  • The round-up now groups renewals by month.
  • Renewal briefs include the last three support notes.
