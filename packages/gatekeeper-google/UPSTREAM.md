Google Workspace: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Chat and BigQuery, with Gmail and
Chat push hooks over Pub/Sub. It can also authenticate users (`providesAuth`).

## What we take

`src/**` (except `src/generated/`), `__tests__/**` and `README.md`, copied by
`scripts/vendor-gatekeepers.mjs`. These are upstream-owned: don't edit them by hand. The
`*-types.txt -> *-types.d.ts` symlinks (the agent-facing API served by `getTypeScriptTypes()`) are
kept as symlinks.

## Local modifications

- **Scope rename.** `@gadgets/*` imports point at `@crm/gatekeeper-interface`,
  `@crm/gatekeeper-kit`, `@crm/gatekeeper-observability` and `@crm/gatekeeper-configurator-ui`
  (including `vi.mock("@gadgets/configurator-ui")` in tests). The 76 source files this touched
  start with a `// Vendored from cloudflare/cloudflare-os@… Modified locally` comment.
- **Worker config.** Upstream generates `wrangler.jsonc` from `cloudflare.config.ts`. We removed
  `cloudflare.config.ts` and keep `wrangler.jsonc` by hand with upstream's settings (compatibility
  date and flags, the `AI` binding, the capnweb-validate build, Text module rules, DO migrations
  `v0`–`v8`) plus: name `crm-gatekeeper-google`, `workers_dev: false`, `preview_urls: false`, and a
  comment listing the secrets. No account IDs.
- **Types.** `worker-configuration.d.ts` is regenerated by `pnpm types`
  (`scripts/gatekeeper-worker-types.mjs`): `mainModule` points back at `src/google.ts`, and
  `restore` is patched onto `cloudflare:workers`, as upstream's generator does.
- **Replaced (ours):** `package.json` (scope, exact versions, Turborepo scripts, `vite` pinned to
  `7.3.6` for the decorator reason given in `../gatekeeper-mcp/UPSTREAM.md`, `typescript6` alias),
  `tsconfig.json` and `tsconfig.test.json` (extend `@crm/config/tsconfig.vendored.json`, no
  `paths`).
- **Test configs.** `vitest.config.ts` (Node) is upstream's, unchanged. `vitest.worker.config.ts`
  and `vitest.docs-worker.config.ts` inline the compatibility date and flags that upstream reads
  from `cloudflare.config.ts` (keep them in step with `wrangler.jsonc`), and take `assert-workerd`
  from `@crm/gatekeeper-interface`.
- **Added** `test/vendor.test.ts` with `vitest.smoke.config.ts`: a workerd smoke test of the
  `GatekeeperVendor` entrypoint against the real `wrangler.jsonc`, with remote bindings off (the
  `AI` binding is remote-only) and `preserveSymlinks` so the `types.txt` links load as text.
- **Removed:** `vite.config.ts` (Vite+ tasks, including `build:configurator` and
  `build:integration-worker`), `deploy-inputs.json` (upstream's deploy-wizard metadata for the
  secrets, now summarised in `wrangler.jsonc`), and `scripts/record-blank-presentation.ts` (a
  maintainer script that records `src/blank-presentation.json` from a live Google account).
