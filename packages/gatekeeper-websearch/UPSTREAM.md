Public web search through Workers AI's `websearch()` and the account's AI Gateway, with the
provider pinned to Ceramic. Auto-provisioned: no connect flow, no secrets, no URL resources. Each
account offers one singleton session, `WebSearchSession`.

## What we take

`src/**`, `__tests__/**`, `README.md` and `AGENTS.md`, copied by `scripts/vendor-gatekeepers.mjs`.
These are upstream-owned: don't edit them by hand. The `types.txt -> types.d.ts` symlink is kept
as a symlink. The README's two images (`../../docs/images/web-search-*.svg`) are not vendored.

## Local modifications

- **Scope rename.** `@gadgets/*` imports point at `@crm/gatekeeper-interface` and
  `@crm/gatekeeper-kit`. The 3 source files this touched start with a
  `// Vendored from cloudflare/cloudflare-os@… Modified locally` comment.
- **Worker config.** Upstream generates `wrangler.jsonc` from `cloudflare.config.ts`. We removed
  `cloudflare.config.ts` and keep `wrangler.jsonc` by hand with upstream's settings (compatibility
  date and flags, observability, the `AI` binding, the capnweb-validate build, Text module rules,
  DO migration `v1`) plus: name `crm-gatekeeper-websearch`, `workers_dev: false`,
  `preview_urls: false`, and a comment on `CF_AI_GATEWAY`. No account IDs.
- **Types.** `worker-configuration.d.ts` is regenerated by `pnpm types`
  (`scripts/gatekeeper-worker-types.mjs`). `src/env.d.ts` adds the optional `CF_AI_GATEWAY`.
- **Replaced (ours):** `package.json` (scope, exact versions, Turborepo scripts, `vite` pinned to
  `7.3.6` for the decorator reason given in `../gatekeeper-mcp/UPSTREAM.md`) and `tsconfig.json`
  (extends `@crm/config/tsconfig.vendored.json`; source and tests in one program, as upstream).
- **Test configs.** `vitest.config.ts` inlines the compatibility date and flags that upstream reads
  from `cloudflare.config.ts` (keep them in step with `wrangler.jsonc`), and takes `assert-workerd`
  from `@crm/gatekeeper-interface` instead of `@gadgets/scripts`.
- **Added** `test/vendor.test.ts` with `vitest.smoke.config.ts`: a workerd smoke test of the
  `GatekeeperVendor` entrypoint and `createAccount()` against the real `wrangler.jsonc`, with
  remote bindings off (the `AI` binding is remote-only).
- **Removed:** `vite.config.ts` (Vite+ task definitions).
