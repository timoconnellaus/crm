An **inbound** email gatekeeper. It is a Cloudflare Email Worker: Email Routing delivers mail to its
`email` handler, which parses it with postal-mime and calls the hook bound to the address's local
part (`<name>@<host>`). It cannot send mail. Stage 6's "email sending for notifications and
digests" needs another route; see `docs/research/gatekeeper-interface.md`.

## What we take

`src/**` (except `src/generated/`) and `README.md`, copied by `scripts/vendor-gatekeepers.mjs`.
These are upstream-owned: don't edit them by hand. The `types.txt -> types.d.ts` symlink is kept
as a symlink.

## Local modifications

- **Scope rename.** `@gadgets/*` imports point at `@crm/gatekeeper-interface`,
  `@crm/gatekeeper-kit`, `@crm/gatekeeper-observability` and `@crm/gatekeeper-configurator-ui`.
  The 3 source files this touched start with a
  `// Vendored from cloudflare/cloudflare-os@… Modified locally` comment.
- **Worker config.** Upstream generates `wrangler.jsonc` from `cloudflare.config.ts`. We removed
  `cloudflare.config.ts` and keep `wrangler.jsonc` by hand with upstream's settings (compatibility
  date and flags, observability, the capnweb-validate build, Text module rules, DO migration `v0`)
  plus: name `crm-gatekeeper-email`, `workers_dev: false`, `preview_urls: false`, and a comment on
  `BASE_URL` and Email Routing. No account IDs and no secrets.
- **Types.** `worker-configuration.d.ts` is regenerated by `pnpm types`
  (`scripts/gatekeeper-worker-types.mjs`).
- **Replaced (ours):** `package.json` (scope, exact versions, Turborepo scripts) and
  `tsconfig.json` (extends `@crm/config/tsconfig.vendored.json`, no `paths`). `tsconfig.test.json`
  adds our smoke test with the Worker pool's types.
- **Added** `test/vendor.test.ts` with `vitest.smoke.config.ts`: a workerd smoke test of the
  `GatekeeperVendor` entrypoint and the fetch handler against the real `wrangler.jsonc`.
- **Removed:** `vite.config.ts` (Vite+ tasks, including `build:configurator`).
