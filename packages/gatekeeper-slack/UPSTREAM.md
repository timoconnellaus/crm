Slack over OAuth (user tokens, with rotation), with three resources: a whole workspace, one
conversation (channel or DM) and one thread.

## What we take

`src/**` (except `src/generated/`) and `README.md`, copied by `scripts/vendor-gatekeepers.mjs`.
Upstream keeps this package's tests next to the source (`src/**/*.test.ts`), so they come with
`src`. These are upstream-owned: don't edit them by hand. The `types.txt -> types.d.ts` symlink is
kept as a symlink.

## Local modifications

- **Scope rename.** `@gadgets/*` imports point at `@crm/gatekeeper-interface`,
  `@crm/gatekeeper-kit` and `@crm/gatekeeper-configurator-ui` (including
  `vi.mock("@gadgets/configurator-ui")` in tests). The 7 source files this touched start with a
  `// Vendored from cloudflare/cloudflare-os@… Modified locally` comment.
- **Worker config.** Upstream generates `wrangler.jsonc` from `cloudflare.config.ts`. We removed
  `cloudflare.config.ts` and keep `wrangler.jsonc` by hand with upstream's settings (compatibility
  date and flags, the capnweb-validate build, Text module rules, DO migration `v0`; upstream sets
  no observability block for this Worker, and nor do we) plus: name `crm-gatekeeper-slack`,
  `workers_dev: false`, `preview_urls: false`, and a comment listing the secrets. No account IDs.
- **Types.** `worker-configuration.d.ts` is regenerated by `pnpm types`
  (`scripts/gatekeeper-worker-types.mjs`).
- **Replaced (ours):** `package.json` (scope, exact versions, Turborepo scripts, `vite` pinned to
  `7.3.6` for the decorator reason given in `../gatekeeper-mcp/UPSTREAM.md`) and `tsconfig.json`
  (extends `@crm/config/tsconfig.vendored.json`, no `paths`). `tsconfig.test.json` adds our smoke
  test with the Worker pool's types.
- **Test configs.** `vitest.config.ts` is upstream's, unchanged.
- **Added** `test/vendor.test.ts` with `vitest.smoke.config.ts`: a workerd smoke test of the
  `GatekeeperVendor` entrypoint against the real `wrangler.jsonc`, with `preserveSymlinks` so the
  `types.txt` link loads as text.
- **Removed:** `vite.config.ts` (Vite+ tasks, including `build:configurator`) and
  `deploy-inputs.json` (upstream's deploy-wizard metadata for the secrets, now summarised in
  `wrangler.jsonc`).
